# ECHO Indications

**Appropriate Use Criteria for transthoracic echocardiography (TTE), online and free.**

Live at **[echoindications.org](https://echoindications.org)**.

The app answers a single question for the requesting clinician:

> *Is a TTE appropriate for this patient, right now, in my setting?*

---

## Who it's for

- **Primary care** clinicians considering an outpatient TTE request.
- **Secondary care inpatient** teams deciding whether an in-hours or out-of-hours TTE is warranted.
- **Secondary care outpatient** clinics planning routine or surveillance imaging.
- **Echo lab triage** accepting or rejecting requests with a citable reason.

---

## What you can do

### Look up an indication
Search the indication library by clinical concept, ECG/CXR finding, or keyword (e.g. *dyspnoea*, *BNP*, *bicuspid*). Each indication shows:

- **GP / IP / OP verdicts** &mdash; colour-coded: green (indicated), red (not indicated), amber (may be considered).
- **Urgency** &mdash; routine, soon, urgent, or "not indicated".
- **Source guideline** &mdash; ASE/ACC, EACVI, BSE, BHVS, or local consensus.
- **Comments** &mdash; rationale and caveats in plain prose.

### Use the Clinical Decision Support tool
If you don't know which indication to look up, start from a **symptom, finding or clinical context** &mdash; chest pain, dyspnoea, palpitations, raised NT-proBNP, post-MI, pre-op, pregnancy, and so on. The tool walks you down a short decision tree to the right indication and shows the verdicts directly.

### Share an indication
Every indication has a unique URL (e.g. `https://echoindications.org/?id=42`). The **Copy link** button drops it into a triage note, an email, or a teaching slide &mdash; clicking the link opens the indication immediately.

### Request a change
Spot something wrong, missing, or out of date? **Right-click an indication &rarr; Request change**, or submit a general issue from any page. Submissions don't require an account.

## Where the content comes from

Indications are paraphrased from published Appropriate Use Criteria:

- **ACC/ASE/AHA AUC** for echocardiography (2011), valvular heart disease (2017), nonvalvular AUC (2019).
- **British Society of Echocardiography** indications and triage statement (2022) and the primary-care triage poster *PUE014* (2024).
- **British Heart Valve Society** / BSE valve disease guidance (2019, 2024).
- **EACVI** appropriateness criteria (2017).
- **ESC** guidelines for heart failure (NICE NG106), pericardial diseases (2015), myocarditis (2023) and ventricular arrhythmias (2022).
- Local consensus where guidelines are silent or contradictory.

Every indication carries its source so you can verify it.
